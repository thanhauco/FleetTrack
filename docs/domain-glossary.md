# Glossary

* **Odometer** - total distance recorded by a vehicle, in kilometres.
* **Service interval** - distance or time after which maintenance is due (10 000 km / 180 days by default).
* **Utilization** - driving hours of completed trips divided by the hours in the reporting period.
* **Full-tank method** - fuel consumption is measured between full fill-ups; the first fill only sets the baseline.
* **Licence class** - category of licence a driver holds (B, C, CE, D).
